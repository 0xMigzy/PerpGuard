The rules underneath all of it. Nothing requires a typed command · every screen has a way back · AUSD amounts in bold, because the money is what a beginner looks for first · money before percentages · each term explained once where it appears · never the word “safe”, always what the liquidation price becomes · no action buttons anywhere a watcher can reach · never offer an amount the person cannot afford.
